DECKARD opens Maschine projects and saves them in Maschine's own format, so you can move
between the two. It can be played from a Maschine MK3 controller or a Novation Launchpad
Pro MK3, and it runs on macOS, Linux and Windows.

It also does a few things Maschine doesn't: expressive recording (MPE, polyphonic
aftertouch and pad pressure recorded as curves on each note), a mod matrix with LFOs and
envelopes, a Launchpad Pro session grid, two synths of its own, and custom themes for the
MK3's screens.
