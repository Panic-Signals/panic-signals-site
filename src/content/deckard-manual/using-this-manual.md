DECKARD is a groovebox for your computer that works like Maschine: scenes and patterns,
a song arrangement, sixteen pads per group, a sampler, drum synths, effects and a
mixer. It opens Maschine projects and saves them in Maschine's own format.

Because DECKARD follows Maschine so closely, NI's own manuals still describe most of
it: the *Maschine 3 Software Manual* for the program and the *Maschine MK3 Manual* for
the controller. This manual doesn't repeat them. It covers:

- **Setting DECKARD up**: installing it, and taking over an MK3 or a Launchpad Pro.
- **What's different**: where DECKARD behaves differently from Maschine, and what it
  doesn't do yet.
- **What's new**: the things Maschine doesn't have.

## How this manual is written

- **Hardware buttons and computer keys** look like this: <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> + <kbd>SELECT</kbd>
  means hold <kbd>SHIFT</kbd> and press <kbd>SELECT</kbd>.
- **Menus** are written with arrows: **File ▸ Export Audio…**.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** are given for macOS. On Windows and Linux, <kbd>⌘</kbd> is
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and <kbd>⌥</kbd> is <kbd>Alt</kbd>.
- **Pads** are numbered 1 to 16 from the bottom left, as on the controller.
- **In the controller diagrams**, cyan marks a button or knob that DECKARD adds to or
  changes. The diagrams are simplified and not to scale.

A box at the top of a chapter names the Maschine manual chapters it builds on.
